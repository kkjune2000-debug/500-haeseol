In Grammar Overview (gstep) rule boxes, condensed "summary-style" explanations — terse dash-lists like "관계에 따라 세 가지 어미 — 같음(~처럼), 더 큼(~보다 더), 덜 함(~보다 덜)" — should be **unpacked into easy explanations**: (1) state the concept / why in plain words, (2) give one concrete example (before → after), (3) keep the English translation paired below.

**Why:** terse cram-summaries are hard for learners; spell out the idea with an example.

**How to apply / exceptions:**
- Do **NOT** expand a rule box whose content just duplicates the table right below it — user: "표 안에 있는 것은 풀어 쓰지 말아라." Leave such table-summaries brief.
- Leave already-clear single-concept boxes (one idea + example) as-is.
- A short framing intro that adds the *concept* (e.g., "하나의 조사가 문맥에 따라 6가지 뜻") is OK even above a table, as long as it doesn't repeat the table's per-row content.
